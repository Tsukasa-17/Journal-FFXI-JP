Put your icons here (PNG, transparent background, for example 64x64):

  available.png   NPC with a quest you can start, or the target of the compass
  locked.png      NPC whose quests wait for another quest
  progress.png    NPC with a quest in progress

The icon is drawn with its bottom edge on the NPC's head, with a short bob.
A missing file leaves the arrow drawn by the addon. After adding or changing
files, run:  /addon reload journal
